# Licensing

Balance is free for personal use. Commercial use requires a separate paid
license from the author.

`LICENSE` holds the binding legal text (PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0).
This file is a plain summary of it and is not a substitute for it. Where the two
appear to disagree, `LICENSE` is what governs.

## What you may do at no cost

Use Balance for any purpose that is not primarily intended for, or directed
toward, commercial advantage or private monetary compensation. In practice that
covers what most people want to do with it:

- track your own money, and your household's
- run it on as many of your own computers as you like
- read, modify, and build the source for your own use
- share modified copies for noncommercial purposes, subject to the license
- use it as a student, or for research or teaching

## What needs a paid license

Anything commercial. Some concrete examples:

- a company using Balance to keep its own books
- an accountant, bookkeeper, or adviser using it for client work
- offering Balance, modified or not, as a hosted or managed service
- bundling it into a product or service you charge for
- internal use inside a business, even without redistributing it

Noncommercial use by an individual does not become commercial merely because
that individual is employed. What matters is whether the use itself serves a
business purpose.

## Buying a commercial license

Email balance.app.support@gmail.com with a short description of your intended use
and roughly how many people would use it. Terms are agreed per customer.

## Third party components

Balance depends on open source packages published under their own licenses,
listed in `package-lock.json`. Those licenses cover those packages. This
project's license covers this project's own source. Balance is source-available
software; PolyForm Noncommercial is not an OSI-approved open source license.
